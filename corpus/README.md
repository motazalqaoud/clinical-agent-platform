# Synthetic guideline corpus

**Not for clinical use.** These documents are a synthetic teaching corpus written for
this repository. They borrow the *structure* of public clinical guidelines (sections,
dosing tables, contraindications) so the retrieval and safety behaviour of the system
can be tested realistically, but the values are illustrative and may be out of date.
Never use them to make a clinical decision.

Point the service at your own institution's approved documents with
`clinagent ingest --corpus /path/to/docs`.
